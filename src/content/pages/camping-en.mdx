Just park your motorhome right next to the gallery entrance. The pitch is lightly paved, there is a meadow beside it, and open fields and plenty of quiet stretch out all around. Stay a night and take it easy. And if you like, I will show you the exhibition: 400 m² in the barn of the historic farmhouse, now converted into a gallery, plus a look into the woodcut workshop. You do not even have to re-park for it.

## Good to know

- One night, free of charge
- Electricity and water are available
- Two pitches, level and lightly paved, quietly located
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- A tour of the gallery and workshop anytime on request
- Small farm shop with regional produce
- Self-sufficient guests: no disposal of chemical toilets or waste water

Two pitches for motorhomes, right next to the house. One night free of charge, with **electricity and water**. A quiet spot, a view into the green, a rustic garden – and it is ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach.

**Like Landvergnügen – only without the membership.** Anyone who calls and asks whether a pitch is free is welcome to come. I make no distinction there.

And if you like, I will gladly show you around the gallery: 400 m² of exhibition space in a converted farmhouse, plus a look into the woodcut workshop. That is how art reaches people who would otherwise never have driven past.

## Good to know

- Two pitches, level and paved, in a quiet spot
- One night, free of charge
- Electricity and water are available
- Self-sufficient guests: no disposal of chemical toilets or waste water
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- A tour of the gallery and the workshop on request

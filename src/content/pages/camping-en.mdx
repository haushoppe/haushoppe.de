Come on by and park your motorhome right next to the gallery entrance. A lightly paved spot, meadow beside it, open fields all around. Stay a night and take it easy.

**Nice and simple:** give me a quick call and ask whether a pitch is free. If it is, just come. Two pitches, that is all a night needs.

**The art comes with it.** If you like, I will show you the gallery: 400 m² in the old farmhouse, the woodcut workshop included. A tour you do not even have to re-park for.

## Good to know

- Two pitches, level and lightly paved, quietly located
- One night, free of charge
- Electricity and water are there
- Self-sufficient guests: no disposal of chemical toilets or waste water
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- A tour of the gallery and workshop anytime on request
- Small farm shop with regional produce

## One phone call is enough

Please give me a call first, as there are only two pitches. If you turn up unannounced, everything may already be taken. And that would be a shame.

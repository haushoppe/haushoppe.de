Park your motorhome right next to the gallery entrance – on the lightly paved pitch, meadow beside it, open fields and quiet all around. And if you like, I will gladly show you the gallery – even right when you arrive.

**It is that simple.** Anyone who calls and asks whether a pitch is free is welcome to come. Two pitches, level and quietly located – that is all you need for a night.

**And then the art.** If you like, I will show you the gallery: 400 m² of exhibition space in a converted farmhouse, plus a look into the woodcut workshop – a tour you do not even have to re-park for.

## Good to know

- Two pitches, level and lightly paved, quietly located
- One night, free of charge
- Electricity and water are available
- Self-sufficient guests: no disposal of chemical toilets or waste water
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- A tour of the gallery and workshop anytime on request
- Small farm shop with regional produce

There are only two pitches – turning up spontaneously is always a risk. Call ahead briefly and your pitch is secure.

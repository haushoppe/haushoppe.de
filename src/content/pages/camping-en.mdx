Campers welcome! Just park your motorhome right next to the gallery entrance. The pitch is lightly paved, there is a small meadow beside it, and open fields and plenty of quiet stretch out all around. Stay a night and take it easy. And if you like, I will show you the exhibition: 400 m² in the barn of the historic farmhouse, now converted into a gallery, plus a look into the woodcut workshop. You do not even have to re-park for it.

## The farm shop

In the entrance area of the gallery you will find our small farm shop. It offers homemade and regional goods: jam, vinegar and fine oils, plus beer, wine and juices. There is the "art beer" in several varieties and the signed "art wine", a little parody where I sign the bottles by hand. In season, apples, fresh apple juice and produce from the garden are added.

## Good to know

- **Rest stop** on private grounds for one night, free of charge
- Mains electricity and drinking water, free of charge
- Two pitches, level and lightly paved, quietly located
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- A tour of the gallery and workshop anytime on request
- Small farm shop: beer, wine, oils, juices and jams
- Self-sufficient guests: no disposal of chemical toilets or grey water

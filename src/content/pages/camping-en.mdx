We have two pitches for motorhomes here. Anyone passing through can stay for one night – free of charge. A lovely view, a rustic garden, and it is ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach. And if you like, I will gladly show you around the gallery. That is how art reaches people who would otherwise never have driven past.

Two pitches, one night, a short phone call beforehand – that is all.

## Good to know

- Two pitches on the gallery grounds, a quiet spot with a view into the green
- One night, i.e. 24 hours, free of charge
- Electricity and water are available
- Self-sufficient guests – no disposal of chemical toilets or waste water
- Ten minutes by bike to the Baltic Sea beach
- On request a personal tour of the exhibition on approx. 400 m² in the converted farmhouse and a look into the woodcut workshop

## Booking

Please give us a short call in advance – there are only two pitches, and turning up spontaneously is always a risk.

## The idea behind it

Many people know the principle from Landvergnügen: farms and direct marketers offer one to three motorhomes a free pitch for one night, and the guests say thank you by buying something in the farm shop. Here the gallery is the farm shop – I also sell directly, just woodcuts instead of agricultural produce. And there is a very small farm shop as well.

You do not have to be a member for this. Anyone who calls and asks whether a pitch is free is welcome to come. I make no distinction there.

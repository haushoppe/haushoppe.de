When goods are bought in the European Union, a legal guarantee of conformity of at least two years from delivery applies by law. It exists independently of the right of withdrawal. The European Union has laid down this harmonised notice for it:

<GuaranteeNotice lang="en" />

### What does this mean in practice?

If a work arrives damaged or does not match its description, please contact us with your order number at [team@haushoppe.de](mailto:team@haushoppe.de). The details are set out in our [terms and conditions](/terms/) (section 9).

Legal basis: Directive (EU) 2024/825, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960.

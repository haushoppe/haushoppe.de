## How to find us

_Close to the Baltic Sea coast, between Wismar and Rostock_

<div class="cp-directions">

<div>

Coming on the A20 from Lübeck, Rostock or Berlin, drive to the Wismar junction and follow the signs to “Insel Poel”.

In Groß-Strömkendorf the road splits into two directions at the hotel Schäfereck. Turn right and you will see the church spire of Dreveskirchen. Follow the main road signs until you have passed the church. Please pay attention to the speed limit of 30 km/h. The nearest town is Stove. At the crossroads turn left and after 100 m follow the sign Haus Hoppe – Galerie für Bildende Kunst.

You can withdraw from a contract you concluded with us online here, without giving any reason, and will then receive a confirmation of receipt by email. If you would rather withdraw informally, write to team@haushoppe.de. Details and the model withdrawal form can be found in our [right of withdrawal](/right-of-withdrawal/).
